How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page usually takes 3–7 days. A full business website or online store takes 2–4 weeks, depending on pages and features.

Should I use WordPress or React / Next.js?

WordPress is great if you want to edit content yourself easily, or need an online store. React and Next.js are best for custom web apps and very fast, SEO-focused sites. I'll suggest the right fit for your project.

Can you build from my Figma or PSD design?

Yes. I convert Figma, Adobe XD or PSD designs into pixel-perfect, fully responsive websites.

Will my website work on mobile?

Every site I build is fully responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Do you help with hosting and maintenance?

Yes. I can set up your domain and hosting, and offer ongoing maintenance, updates and fixes after launch.
