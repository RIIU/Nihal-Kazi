FAQ

Questions,
answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Send me a message.

How long does a typical project take?

A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks. A full SaaS product or mobile app takes 4–8 weeks depending on the number of screens and complexity.

What do I need to get started?

Just your idea and goals. If you have brand assets, competitors you like, or existing designs, share them too. We'll figure out the rest together on a call.

Do you also develop the website or app?

My focus is design, but I provide developer-ready Figma files and can recommend trusted developers for the build.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes multiple rounds of revisions. The goal is that you're 100% happy with the final result.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work with clients worldwide and adjust my schedule to overlap with your time zone for calls.
